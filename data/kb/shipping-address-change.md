id: shipping-address-change
title: Changing the delivery address or cancelling an order
topic: shipping
---

You can change the delivery address or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, from the Orders page.
After 1 hour the order is sent to the warehouse and the address can no longer be changed.
If an order has already been dispatched, you can ask the carrier to redirect it using the tracking link.
We cannot deliver to parcel lockers or PO boxes for express orders.
