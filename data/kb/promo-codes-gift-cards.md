title: Promo codes and gift cards
topic: billing
---

Only one promo code can be used per order.
Promo codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed.
Gift cards are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase.
You can check a gift card balance from the Gift Cards page.
Lost gift cards can be replaced only if you have the original purchase receipt.
