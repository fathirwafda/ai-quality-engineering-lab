Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.95.
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $12.95.
Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day are dispatched the same day.
We do not deliver on public holidays.
