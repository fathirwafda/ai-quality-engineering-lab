---
id: order-tracking
title: Tracking an order, delays and lost packages
topic: shipping
---

A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order is dispatched.
You can also track an order from the Orders page in your account.
Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.
If your package has not arrived 5 business days after the expected delivery date, contact us and we will open a carrier investigation.
If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund.
