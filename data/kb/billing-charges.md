title: Unexpected charges, double charges and invoices
topic: billing
---

If you see two charges for one order, one is usually a temporary authorisation hold that disappears within 3 to 5 business days.
If both charges are still there after 5 business days, contact us with your order number and we will refund the duplicate charge.
A tax invoice is attached to your order confirmation email and can be downloaded from the Orders page.
Prices include sales tax where it applies.
