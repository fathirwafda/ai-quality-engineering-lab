id: refunds-timeline
title: When you get your refund
topic: returns
---

Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the returned item reaches our warehouse.
Inspecting a return takes up to 3 business days after it arrives.
After we issue a refund, your bank can take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
The original shipping cost is not refunded unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.
