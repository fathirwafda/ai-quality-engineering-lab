title: Damaged, faulty or wrong items
topic: returns
---

If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, tell us within 7 days of delivery.
Send a photo of the item and the packaging from the Orders page.
We send a free replacement or a full refund, including the shipping cost.
You do not need to return a damaged item unless we ask you to.
