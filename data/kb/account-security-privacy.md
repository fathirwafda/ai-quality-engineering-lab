title: Account security, privacy and deleting your account
topic: account
---

If you think someone else has accessed your account, reset your password immediately and contact support.
Support will never ask for your password or your full card number.
You can download a copy of your personal data from Account settings.
You can ask us to delete your account from Account settings; deletion is completed within 30 days.
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email.
