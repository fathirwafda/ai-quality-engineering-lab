title: Warranty, stock and restocks
topic: product
---

Electronics and home appliances come with a 12 month warranty against manufacturing faults.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage or normal wear and tear.
To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the fault.
Out of stock products show a Notify me button that emails you when the product is back in stock.
We cannot reserve out of stock products or share exact restock dates.
