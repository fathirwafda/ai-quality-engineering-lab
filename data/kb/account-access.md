title: Signing in, password reset and two-step verification
topic: account
---

To reset your password, select Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you.
Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.
You can turn on two-step verification in Account settings to protect your account.
If you no longer have access to your email address, contact support to verify your identity.
