title: Talking to a person and how escalation works
topic: other
---

You can ask to speak to a person at any time and we will pass your conversation to a support agent.
Support agents are available Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm.
Reports of fraud, unauthorised charges or a compromised account are always handled by a support agent.
Chargebacks, legal complaints and threats are handled by our customer relations team.
A support agent replies within one business day.
